THE MATHEMATICAL ARCHITECTURE

From position to checkable explanation.

The live application instantiates five concrete structural predicates from Section 3, computes the local repair profile of Section 4, and exhaustively enumerates immediate legal opponent replies for a bounded persistence test. Move legality and predicates are recomputed from the same local kernel; a separate rules-engine conformance audit and deeper strategy certification remain subsequent development steps.
