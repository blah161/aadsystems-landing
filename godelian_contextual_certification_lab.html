05 · What this does—and what it doesn't

Established by this program

Finite semantic behavior

The model exhausts all Boolean formulas on two atoms (16 truth tables) and all presentations containing zero, one or two axioms (153 presentations). Its contextual tests characterize equality of model sets in this small classical universe.

Because the chosen successor is determined only by the model set, equal presentations stay equivalent here. This toy system cannot display genuine delayed Gödelian divergence.

Requires separate mathematics

Actual Gödelian verification

The manuscript concerns arithmetized provability, uniform PA-internal proof certificates, diagonalization and inherited-axiom Gödelian successors.

Those require explicit proof encodings and Lean-checked arithmetic theorems. The companion Lean project tracks the outstanding proof obligations; the browser model does not discharge them.
